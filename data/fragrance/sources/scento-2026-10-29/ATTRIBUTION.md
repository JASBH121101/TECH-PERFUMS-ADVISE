## Changes made by JBAE

JBAE preserves the source CSV files and creates a normalized Fragrance-domain representation. It maps concentration labels where the source term has an unambiguous JBAE equivalent, maps the source heart tier to `middleNotes`, keeps the Scento family taxonomy explicitly labeled, and creates field-scoped evidence records. Missing source values are not filled.

The JBAE evidence status for imported claims is `reported`; JBAE has not independently verified each claim. Scento does not endorse JBAE or JB Parfums.
